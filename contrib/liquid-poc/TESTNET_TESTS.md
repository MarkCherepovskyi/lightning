## Notes

- **Bugs found by this run** (all fixed on `liquid-merged`): the fee-asset byte order of `liquid-testnet` (`e63b91127`, unit test `8f72bafe6`); the `withdrawasset` crash (`ff9fc9fc6`); the bookkeeper underflow for a swept `to_remote` (`89dda8d11`).
- **T7 needed a test-database fix.** Alice's wallet still held a phantom L-BTC coin from the invalid close tx of the stuck channel (`aab614c4`, never mined). The HTLC-timeout builder picked it as its fee input and the network rejected the tx (`bad-txns-inputs-missingorspent`) until I deleted the row. Gap: an asset spend should not keep choosing an unconfirmed coin from a tx that never confirms. Her wallet also still lists the matching phantom asset output (unconfirmed), which `withdrawasset` ignores.
- **Dave's bookkeeper** was left disabled (`disable-plugin=bookkeeper` in his config) because his `9800d5f6`/`41417911` account data was recorded before the fix and stays underflowed. A node that never ran the bad build does not have this.
- **HTLC hold.** `--dev-disconnect` did not hold an HTLC reliably (the payment completed in under a second). The cases use a 40 s delay plugin on the payee, and stop the other side while the HTLC is committed.
- **Public mempool.** On the real network `lnpoc.py close` must use the txid returned by `close`, not "the first new mempool tx".
- **Stuck:** alice↔bob `467cbe5d` (opened before the byte-order fix): 1,000,000 units and 20,000 sat in a 2-of-2 whose closing tx has the wrong asset id (T14).
- **Not covered:** blinded outputs; channels announced and then force-closed; interop with nodes that do not run this branch; fee estimation over time (testnet fees are near the minimum).
