Announced: after 6 confirmations every node learns it through gossip and can route through it. Private: only these two nodes know it, so other payers can use it only as the last hop of an invoice; routes of 3 hops or more need announced channels.

Pay

Finds a path over open channels on the same network. Several hops are fine.

The invoice shows in Activity, with a button to copy it.

After a close, each side's share goes to its node's wallet: watch Wallet on the node card. A cooperative close pays both sides as soon as the closing tx confirms. A force close pays the peer at once and the closing node after the to_self_delay (24 h on public chains).

Force close makes the node fail the channel and broadcast its commitment; each side then sweeps its outputs as the timelocks pass. Where the page can mine, it mines those blocks.

evm-withdraw: pays the node's ETH address what the contract holds for it.

Cash out

Exit everything in the wallet, wait until the contract credits it, then Withdraw. The log shows the ETH balance before and after.

Did the money arrive?

  1. In a channel: the Channels table splits the capacity (left: the first node). After a payment the Activity log shows the route and who forwarded it.
  2. After a close: the close's log gives your share and the wallet before and after. The node card's Wallet grows when the closing tx is at the safe block (a few minutes). After a force close the closing node waits the to_self_delay (24 h here); the channel's state column shows onchaind's countdown.
  3. Exit: the wallet's sat leave Lightning and the card's Withdrawable grows by the same amount (in ETH). CLN keeps 25,000 sat back while it still knows anchor channels.
  4. Withdraw: the contract pays the ETH address. The log shows the ETH balance before and after, and Gas on the card goes up.
  5. On the explorer: the ETH address link on the card opens Blockscout: the withdraw shows as a transaction to UtxoChain with an internal transfer to the address. Funding and closing txs link from the channel rows and the logs.
  6. The sum: gas balance + withdrawable + wallet + channel shares stays what you deposited, less the EVM gas and the Lightning fees paid.
